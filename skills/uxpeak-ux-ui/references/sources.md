## Critical departures from the channel

The skill preserves useful task clarity, hierarchy and prototyping techniques while rejecting deceptive examples. In particular, conversion-oriented examples in V03–V05 do not justify fake urgency, invented social proof, pressure through threatened losses, misleading price comparisons or silent subscription enrollment. Claimed uplifts remain claims.

V08 and V22 contain contextual or conflicting numerical examples. Current applicable accessibility and platform requirements take precedence over tutorial shortcuts. V09's elaborate controls are alternatives to evaluate, not a scale of designer seniority. V14's preference for high fidelity does not replace choosing the right fidelity for the question. Historical visual trends and software workflows require fresh verification.

Accessibility, truthful commitment flows, responsive acceptance checks and implementation safeguards in this skill include supplemental professional judgment. They are not all statements made by UXpeak.
